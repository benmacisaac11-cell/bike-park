Insurance rule: check on arrival

Lock through the frame to an immovable object:

  • steel or concrete, at least 4 cm thick (about two fingers wide)
  • fixed into concrete or stone: no bolts you could undo, no loose rack
  • a shape the bike can't be lifted over (a closed hoop, not a short post)
  • never left out for more than 24 hours

The app can't measure a hoop. Green means the data says it is a proper steel stand; confirm the three points above when you get there, then tap "Good hoop" so it's remembered.

Data: OpenStreetMap contributors (includes racks verified on I Rode Here), City of Melbourne open data. Street View opens in Google Maps.
