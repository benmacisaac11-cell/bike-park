The lock-up rule
Your insurer needs
  • A lock on your insurer's approved list, under 2 years old when the cover started.
  • The lock goes through the frame and around the stand. A wheel alone does not count.
  • The stand is steel or concrete at least 40 mm thick (a golf ball), fixed into concrete or stone.
  • Nothing on it comes apart with a spanner or Allen key.
  • The bike cannot be lifted over the top or slid out underneath.
  • Sign poles, trees, wire fences and wheel-slot racks do not count.
  • Inside a bike cage, still lock through the frame to a fixed steel rail.
  • Back at the bike within 24 hours.
  • At a café stop, the bike within 5 m and in your sight counts as attended.
  • For a theft claim: a police report, a lock key, the bike's receipt and proof it was locked.
Worth doing every time
  • Photo of the locked bike before you walk away. That is your proof.
  • Take the display with you.
